## PROCESS

I started by baking a traditional Czech gingerbread cake on a baking tray. The next morning, I was planning to decorate it with chocolate glaze, but then I got the idea to use it for my letterform assignment instead.

I decided to carve the letter K directly into the gingerbread. After cutting out the shape, I carefully transferred it onto a sheet of paper to photograph it. During the process, the letter broke slightly, leaving some imperfections and small cracks that became part of its final appearance.

I took several photographs of the finished letter before eventually eating it for breakfast the following morning.
